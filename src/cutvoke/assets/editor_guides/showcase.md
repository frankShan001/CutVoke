# Promotion, product demonstration and showcase

Layer 2. Use for a product or brand promo, feature demonstration, portfolio reel or promotional montage. For music-led expression also load the music-video reference when needed.

Infer the audience's question and the single thing they should remember or do. Build an appropriate path through a problem, visible demonstration, benefit/result and ending. These are optional story functions, not a compulsory four-part template. Use real UI/action/product evidence for claims; identify any illustrative or simulated shot honestly.

Give each shot a role: orient, prove, reveal, compare or conclude. Keep feature demonstrations readable long enough to understand the action and resulting change. A fast reel can still have one clear focal point per passage. Avoid relying on impressive language to explain a benefit missing from the images.

Design graphics, typography, motion and transitions around the reveal, action direction or information hierarchy. For a high-impact brief, prototype a memorable reveal or repeatable visual device and compare it with a plain version. Check whether the treatment increases attention to the product and comprehension of the benefit. For a measured brief, use the same test to justify quieter choices.

Check brand/name/copy, actual product behavior, text safe areas and contrast, voice/music balance and the intended ending. Effects, generated illustrations or stock clips cannot establish an unsupported factual claim. Use only authorized assets and services; no upload, paid generation or publication is implied by a request to edit.

Review the first-view impression and the complete path from opening to payoff. Point to the actual shot demonstrating the claim and the passage delivering the requested style. Record unresolved sensory judgments rather than assigning an automatic polish score.

Research context: [editing-quality dimensions for AI-generated cinematic ads](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24329). Its small, ad-specific sample informs applicable review questions, not a universal rubric or a guarantee of conversion.
