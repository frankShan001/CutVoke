## English cinematic families (2026-10-17)

Unmodified binaries from the official Google Fonts repository, pinned to commit
`47d54ca1ebb14fd864695f1d39dc734f39017c33`. Exact URLs and SHA-256 hashes are
recorded in `latin-font-provenance.json`.

Barlow Condensed (Regular/Bold), Space Grotesk (variable), Cormorant Garamond
(variable), and IBM Plex Mono (Regular/Bold) ship with their original family
OFL license files. Cache font instances are internal render derivatives;
the shipped source binaries are unchanged.
